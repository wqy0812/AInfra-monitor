## Serial publication fix (2026-09-26)

The user identified a real ordering defect: preparing CPU/DCU/A3 in advance adds later datasources and invalidates earlier full snapshots even when panels have not changed. Publishing an earlier batch also changes the snapshot needed by later batches. Keep the full-resource concurrency checks; serialize complete batches instead.

The admission driver now returns at the first ready batch. Repeated invocation waits while that batch is pending publication or observation. After observation passes, rollback completes, or admission failure is recorded, rerun the same command to continue with a fresh snapshot for the next group. `prepare` also rejects another unfinished sibling transaction, under a file lock, before capturing a snapshot or creating a datasource. Existing stale snapshots are never rewritten to fake acceptance; retry them with fresh evidence.
